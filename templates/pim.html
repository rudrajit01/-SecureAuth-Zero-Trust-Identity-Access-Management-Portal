Why Privileged Identity Management?

  • Standing access is a security risk: Permanent administrator accounts significantly increase the blast radius if compromised.
  • Just-In-Time (JIT) access: Privileges are granted only for the requested duration and automatically expire.
  • Step-up MFA & Justification: Requires business rationale and immediate multi-factor re-verification.
  • Full Audit Trail: Every activation is logged to SIEM telemetry with security alerts.
